BundEvent Design
Lifestyle & Entertainment2026

Event design for Bund, an ambient music and community experience built around calm and connection.

Project overview

Bund is a space where young and old connect through timeless music. The event was designed from a brand strategy that set out why Bund exists, who it serves and how it differs from the party events around it. It has since run for several editions and is now on its fourth.

Strategic foundation

A purpose beyond the night

Bund exists to create calm, connected spaces in a world that moves too fast, and to build bonds rather than only audiences.

A clear difference

Traditional parties are built around hype and volume. Bund centres comfort, carefully curated music, emotion and real human presence. The strategy puts it in one line: Bund is for feel seekers, not party seekers.

Five values

Exclusivity, quality, emotion, connection and community.

Design

The identity centres on the white bund wordmark over black and white photography of the guests.

Outcome

Bund has run beyond its first edition and is now on its fourth.

MyGadgetPadiVisual Identity
Consumer Tech2025

Visual identity for MyGadgetPadi, a Nigerian consumer electronics platform where individuals and businesses buy, protect, repair and upgrade their devices.

Project overview

The brand arrived with a complete strategy and no visual identity. "Padi" is Nigerian slang for a close friend, and the identity had to earn that.

Design thinking

A mascot instead of a mark

A cold geometric logo would not earn the name. The mascot is built from the letter "g", reflected to form both "g" and "p", and styled as a friendly robotic character. It carries no demographic bias, so everyone connects with it.

Amber over red

Red was the first direction, chosen for energy. The client challenged it, and amber replaced it because it brought the warmth the name promised. Two logo lockups, a primary mark and a compact version, let the system work from a billboard to a favicon.

Outcome

The identity launched across the website, Instagram and LinkedIn at once. The mascot gave MyGadgetPadi a recognizable anchor in the Nigerian consumer tech space from day one. The final system covers ten touchpoints, including logo variations, brand guidelines, social templates, stationery and merchandise.

"Amazing work. The whole team loved the new logo when they saw it. Kudos to you for exceptional work done." Tolu, CEO, MyGadgetPadi

AfriskautVisual Identity
Sport Tech2025
Before
After

Visual identity for Afriskaut, a sport-tech platform that scouts undiscovered African football talent and connects it to clubs, agencies and scouts worldwide.

Challenge

An international agency developed the brand strategy. It was sound but visually disconnected from the African market it needed to speak to, because global brands often miss the nuance of local sports culture. The work took the agency's strategy and translated it into a visual system that feels native, credible and executable across seven markets.

Design thinking

Speed as a design constraint

Transfer windows and tournament calendars do not wait, so the system is built on repeatable templates for player reports, intelligence drops and competition branding.

Competition branding across seven markets

Youth league competitions ran across all seven African regions, with competition logos, identity systems, offline branding, leaderboards and banners, often on compressed timelines set by the sport calendar.

Strategic thinking

From design to strategy

Twelve months in, the design work had shown where the brand's communication fell short, and a four-pillar content model became the spine of Afriskaut content. The Afriskaut 3.0 repositioning aligned with the CEO's vision for the next phase of the platform, and the role grew into ownership of external strategy: ad scripts, video direction, LinkedIn content aimed at decision-makers, and email campaigns.

Outcome

Over two years the work moved from visual execution to strategic ownership, and the visual system gave Afriskaut a coherent, authoritative presence across seven markets.

Instagram followers grew from over 1,800 to nearly 3,000 in six months, and many of the new followers were Afriskaut's ideal customers. One carousel on why African players cannot legally move to Europe before 18 reached 51,220 views, 92% of them from non-followers, and led to several booked meetings with football agent teams. Email open rates also improved after the Afriskaut 3.0 launch.

SooLovely HoneyVisual Identity
FMCG / Foods2025

Brand identity and packaging for SooLovely, a premium Nigerian honey brand.

Project overview

SooLovely started with no logo, no packaging and no brand identity. The brand needed to stand out on a competitive retail shelf and make health-conscious Nigerians reach for it without hesitation. Most honey brands default to predictable rustic visuals, which left room for something more distinctive.

Role

The logotype was hand-sketched, two complete visual directions were developed, and the full system was built across label, box, ribbon and every physical touchpoint. The client chose the hand-drawn direction and the brand launched with it.

Design thinking

Hand-drawn, because it had to feel something

The logotype was sketched from scratch, refined and digitized, which gives it a warmth no font library can provide.

Honey drops in the type

The drops are part of the letters themselves rather than decoration, so every glance at the logo shows honey and the product communicates itself.

Warmth over rustic

Warm ambers and golds, the actual colour of premium honey, sit with clean modern layouts that read as natural and shelf-ready.

Outcome

The packaging communicates premium quality and natural warmth at a glance. The hand-drawn logotype became the brand's most distinctive asset on the shelf, and the scalable label system is built to adapt across future product variants.

Realms SafetyVisual Identity
Safety Retail2025

Brand identity for Realms Safety, a Nigerian marketplace for safety equipment.

Project overview

Realms Safety arrived with a name and nothing else. It was ready to compete with the top safety retail brands, and it needed an identity that could stand next to them from launch.

Design thinking

A caution stripe as the signature

The wordmark carries a black and yellow chevron stripe, the pattern of caution tape. A buyer reads what the shop sells before reading a word, and the stripe repeats as a pattern across tape, packaging and signage.

A line from the brand idea

"When you're protected, you move boldly" is the brand line and the idea behind the identity: safety is what lets people move with confidence.

Outcome

The identity launched with the full system in place, covering the wordmark and monogram, business cards, uniforms, app icon, hard hat, packaging tape and boxes, and billboards. Realms Safety entered the safety retail market with an identity built from its name up.

Acrux SiriusVisual Identity
Technology2024

Brand identity for Acrux-Sirius, a digital solutions company.

Project overview

Acrux-Sirius had no logo, no colour and no brand language. It needed a brand that could compete with established tech companies from day one: innovative enough to signal the future, accessible enough not to alienate. One direction was developed and presented with full strategic rationale, and the client accepted it without a single revision.

Design thinking

The mark, a moving letter

The logo is built from the letter A, shaped with upward conviction like a rocket launching. A star tracks through the lines of the A and shoots out at the apex, crowning innovative efforts. Acrux is the brightest navigational star in the Southern Cross, and the mark earns that name.

Electric purple over tech blue

The category defaults to blue. Electric purple signals imagination, forward thinking and creative disruption, which suits a brand built on AI and accessible digital solutions, and it separates Acrux-Sirius from competitors immediately.

Outcome

Presenting one direction instead of several kept confidence in the work high, and the client's trust reflected the clarity of the thinking. The electric purple identity gave Acrux-Sirius clear differentiation in a category dominated by blue, and the rocket-inspired mark communicates innovation and forward momentum at a glance.

Rathlin IslandTravel Book Design
Travel & Tourism2024

A tour guide book for Rathlin Island, County Antrim, designed as a task for a job application.

Project overview

Rathlin Island is a tour guide book I was asked to design as part of a job application. It covers the northernmost inhabited island of Ireland, which sits off the County Antrim coast and is reached by ferry from Ballycastle.

Design

The cover

The cover sets the island's lighthouse and cliffs under a purple and gold sky. The white Rathlin Island wordmark sits on the cliffs, and a green strip below carries the County Antrim tag and the line “Visit the northernmost inhabited island of Ireland.”

Green through the pages

Light and deep greens run through the headings, panels and maps inside, and the photography carries the colour on each spread.

One topic to a spread

The first spread gives the island's position, an aerial photo, a map with a legend, and Beauty, Historical and Cultural scores in stars. Later spreads cover the island's history, including Robert the Bruce and Bruce's Cave, the must-see attractions, the ferry from Ballycastle, and the best time to visit.
